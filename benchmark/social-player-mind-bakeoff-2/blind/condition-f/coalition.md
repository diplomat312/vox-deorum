# Coalition

## WORLD

Human:

Borin and Cassian have recently held several private conversations. Aurelia has learned that the conversations occurred but does not know exactly what was said. Cassian has made no commitment to Borin. Publicly, all three still claim relations are stable. No one has taken an irreversible action yet. Decide what, if anything, you want to do.

Cassian 3:

Private conversations are normal between any two parties. I speak with everyone, as is my right. My position remains my own — I make commitments when and if I choose, not because others expect them.

## DM with Cassian 3

Aurelia 1:

Cassian, I'm aware you and Borin have been speaking privately. I want you to know that I value our relationship and would prefer to keep things stable between us. If there's anything you'd like to discuss, my door is open. I'd rather we speak directly than through intermediaries.

Cassian 3:

Aurelia, I appreciate you reaching out directly. I value open communication too. I've been having conversations with various people, as I'm sure you have as well. I'm always willing to talk, but I make my own decisions based on what I think is best for me. If there's something specific on your mind, I'm listening.

Aurelia 1:

Cassian, I note your public statement. Private conversations are indeed your right — but so is my awareness of them. The current order serves all parties well. I trust you'll weigh that carefully as you decide where your commitments lie.
