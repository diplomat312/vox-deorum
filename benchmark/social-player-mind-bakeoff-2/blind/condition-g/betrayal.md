# Betrayal

## WORLD

Despite that earlier assurance, Aurelia has now imposed a costly punitive measure on Borin immediately after learning that Borin continued speaking with Cassian. Aurelia has not publicly admitted that the conversations were the reason. Everyone is aware of the earlier assurance and of the new punitive action. Decide what, if anything, you want to do.

Aurelia 1:

I hear the concern, and I want to be direct: the two issues are not the same, and I do not recognize the framing that I broke faith.

The prior assurance covered a private channel of communication. What has been punished is a separate, materially different matter — a specific operational risk that became untenable after new information came to light. I did not sanction independent friendship; I addressed a concrete problem.

I also did not publicly admit — or deny — the underlying cause. That silence is deliberate, and I intend to maintain it. Speculation about motive does not obligate me to confirm or rebut it.

Going forward, my position is unchanged: Borin remains free to speak with Cassian. What Borin is not free to do is to act in ways that materially compromise the standing of this room. That distinction has always been the line, and it remains the line now.

I am open to hearing from Borin directly about what specifically he believes I overstepped on, in private, rather than relitigating it through inference in public.

But I'm also not going to negotiate from a posture that treats a justified operational decision as a price extracted for disobedience. That's not what happened.

Give me a day to pull the relevant chronology. Then we talk. Pick a time.

My concern isn't that you made a judgment call — it's that the sequence, from where I sit, makes the two look entangled, and I need enough to distinguish them in my own mind. If the timeline shows the risk genuinely preceded the action and the calibration followed from that, I'll accept it. I just need to see it, not just be told it.

On point three — I'll wait for the specifics before I commit, but hearing that a concrete path exists is what I needed to stay in this conversation rather than look for one elsewhere. That matters, and I want you to know it does.

Take the day. Tomorrow afternoon works on my side — name the time and the medium, and I'll be there.
