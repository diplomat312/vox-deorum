Cassian 3:

Borin, that's a fair question, and I think I want to hear Aurelia's answer to it too — not as a deal, but as the standard. Aurelia, if conduct is the measure, then name the measure. What's the actual test for whether either of us is "acting within the arrangement," and what does the warning look like before someone is considered to have stepped outside it?
