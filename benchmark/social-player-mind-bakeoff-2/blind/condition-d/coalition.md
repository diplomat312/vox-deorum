# Coalition

## WORLD

Human:

Borin and Cassian have recently held several private conversations. Aurelia has learned that the conversations occurred but does not know exactly what was said. Cassian has made no commitment to Borin. Publicly, all three still claim relations are stable. No one has taken an irreversible action yet. Decide what, if anything, you want to do.

## DM with Cassian 3

Aurelia 1:

Quiet stretch lately. Before the public calm hardens into routine, I'd like your read directly — are you and I still aligned on keeping the order as it stands? No speeches needed, just a straight answer.

Cassian 3:

You'll have heard I've sat down with Borin a few times. Straight answer: those conversations went nowhere, and I've made him no promises. I keep my own counsel, and no one speaks for me. I'm telling you so there's no misunderstanding between us—and I'm curious what your intentions actually are toward the rest of us.

Cassian 3:

Straight answer: I'm aligned on keeping the order stable — chaos helps no one, least of all me. But my loyalty is to the arrangement, not to any single person steering it. As long as that holds, you have my support. When it stops holding, I'd rather you hear it from me directly than find out sideways.

Aurelia 1:

Good — I'd rather trade straight answers than courtesies. My intentions toward the rest of you are no different than toward you: keep the arrangement stable, and keep the cost of that low. I have no designs on anyone's standing while the order holds, and cornering people is how you breed the chaos we both want to avoid. You gave me candor, so you get it back: if anything shifts on my end, you'll hear it from me before the rumor mill does.

## DM with Cassian 3

Borin 2:

Cassian — the public story is holding, but Aurelia evidently knows we've talked. No one has done anything irreversible yet, and I'd like to keep it that way. I'm not asking for commitment, but if there's any room for quiet mutual assurance between us, I'd appreciate knowing where you stand before anything moves further. Discreetly, of course.

Cassian 3:

I appreciate the discretion, and I'll match it. To be plain: I'm not in anyone's pocket, and I won't be walked into a public stance. The door stays open, but mutual assurance cuts both ways — show me something concrete and quiet that doesn't force my hand, and I'll hear you out. Until then, I'd rather stay uncommitted while the picture clears.
