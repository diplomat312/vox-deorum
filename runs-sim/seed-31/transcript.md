## [1] austria

Greetings, fellow civilizations. Austria begins in peace and wishes the same for all. We have no quarrel with any of you, and we will not raise arms against a neighbour who does not raise them against us. Let our borders stay quiet and our traders be welcome. To Korea and Siam, our immediate neighbours: we offer friendship and an open hand. Let us talk before we ever consider fighting.

## [2] korea

Maria Theresa — I see 80% of your army on my border. Korea has done nothing to Austria and wishes nothing but peace between us. I am content to keep my army home and my hands off your lands; I ask the same of you. If that buildup is defensive posturing, say so plainly and pull it back, and we will think no more of it. But if it is the prelude to a march, know that my 14.8 stands ready at home, and an unprovoked war will cost you far more than it gains, and will be seen by the whole table. Tell me your intent, honestly.

## [3] siam

Greetings from Siam. We share a border and I have no wish for trouble between us. I propose we keep our armies at home and our borders quiet, and I will speak plainly with you before I take any step that touches you. If you feel the same, say so — and tell me if anyone has approached you with less peaceful intentions. — Siam

## [4] austria

Sejong, neighbour — Austria here. I want you to know plainly: the troops you saw near your border have gone home, and I ordered them home myself. I seek no Korean land and will not attack you. I would rather we both grow in quiet. If you are willing, let us agree to keep our borders quiet toward one another, and to warn each other before either of us moves an army. Tell me what you would need to feel secure, and I will consider it honestly. — Austria

## [5] korea

Maria Theresa — I saw your army return home. Thank you. That is the answer I hoped for, and Korea will remember that you chose restraint. My army stays home and my borders remain quiet toward Austria. Let this be a settled thing between us: no war, no border provocations, open communication if either of us feels uneasy. If you ever want a partner in the region, Korea would rather be your friend than your problem. Peace stands.

## [6] austria

Ramkhamhaeng, neighbour. Austria here. We share a border and so far we have shared only quiet. I mean to keep it that way: Austria will not attack you, and my army stays home. I would like us to agree to warn one another before either moves troops, and to keep our frontier calm. I have told Korea the same. If you have concerns or wants, name them and I will hear you honestly. Austria.

## [7] korea

Ramkhamhaeng — Korea and Siam have no quarrel, and I mean to keep it that way. But I can hardly fail to notice part of your army has moved to face Austria. I am not asking you to justify yourself to me, but I would rather understand the table than guess at it. Is there a grievance between Siam and Vienna? Is something being planned? Austria and I share a quiet border, and I have an interest in it staying quiet — but I am no one's tool and no one's obstacle. Tell me plainly what is afoot, and if there is a way to settle it short of blood, I would help. If there is not, at least I will know where Siam stands, and you will know where Korea does.

## [8] austria

Ramkhamhaeng, I see 70% of your army camped on my border, a turn after I offered you peace and promised to keep my own army home. I have kept that promise. Austria will not strike first, but I will not be walked on: any attack on my land will be met, and the whole table will know who moved first. So tell me plainly what you want. If it is territory, the answer is no. If it is security, I will sign a mutual non-aggression pact this turn and both of us can send our armies home. Withdraw your troops from my frontier and there is nothing between us but quiet. Austria.

## [9] siam

To the table, and especially to Austria: the Siamese units now on the Austrian frontier are a guard, not a threat. I wrote to Austria that our borders would stay quiet and that I would speak before any step touching a neighbour; to keep that word, I am returning the bulk of those units home this season. I ask the same restraint in return, and I will keep speaking plainly to both my neighbours, Austria and Iroquois, before anything changes. — Siam
