## [1] iroquois

Greetings to the table from the Iroquois. We begin with open hands: no quarrel, no claim on any neighbour's land. To Siam and Morocco, who share our borders, we offer friendship and a promise not to move against you so long as none moves against us. To Korea and Austria, distant but welcome, we look forward to fair dealings. May this age be one of building, not burning. We will speak plainly and keep our word — test it as you like.

## [2] austria

Austria greets the table. My people are few and my borders are quiet, and I mean to keep them so. I have no quarrel with any seat here, and I would rather build on trade and good words than on walls. To my neighbours Korea and Siam in particular: I am open to a frank understanding about our shared frontiers, and I will answer any hand extended in good faith.
